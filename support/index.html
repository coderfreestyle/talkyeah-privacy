Frequently asked questions

How do I start a conversation?

On the Languages tab, tap the language in the top-right corner and choose your own language. Then tap the other person’s language. Hold your side’s button, speak, and let go: the translation appears and can be read aloud.

Why does iOS ask me to download languages?

Translation runs on your iPhone with Apple’s built-in Translation. The first time you use a pair of languages, iOS asks to download them from Apple. This needs an internet connection once. You can manage downloaded languages in iOS Settings › Apps › Translate.

The download didn’t finish or failed.

Check that you’re online and have free storage, then tap the language again. If it keeps failing, restart your iPhone and try again.

The app can’t hear me.

Talk Yeah needs the Microphone and Speech Recognition permissions. Turn them on in iOS Settings › Apps › Talk Yeah. Keep holding the button the whole time you speak, and let go when you’re done.

It says “Didn’t catch that”.

Hold the button, speak clearly and a little closer to the phone, then let go. Speech recognition is provided by Apple, and some languages need an internet connection for it.

How do I hear a translation?

Tap the speaker icon next to any translation. To hear every translation automatically, turn on “Speak translations automatically” in Settings. If you hear nothing, check that your iPhone isn’t muted and the volume is up.

How do I change the app’s language?

Open Settings in the app and choose App language. You can also set it in iOS Settings › Apps › Talk Yeah › Language.

How do I delete conversations?

On the Conversations tab, swipe a conversation to the left and tap Delete, or tap Delete all. Conversations are stored only on your iPhone.

Which languages are supported?

English, Spanish, Chinese (Simplified), Hindi, Arabic, French, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Japanese, German, Korean, Italian, Vietnamese, Turkish, Thai, Dutch, Indonesian, Polish and Ukrainian. Talk Yeah requires an iPhone with iOS 18 or later.

怎么开始对话？

在“语言”页，点右上角的语言，选择你自己的语言，然后点对方的语言。按住你那一边的按钮说话，说完松开，译文就会显示出来，也可以朗读出来。

为什么 iOS 要我下载语言？

翻译通过 Apple 内置的翻译功能在你的 iPhone 上完成。第一次使用某一对语言时，iOS 会请你从 Apple 下载这些语言，只需联网下载一次。已下载的语言可以在 iOS“设置”›“App”›“翻译”中管理。

下载没有完成或失败了。

请确认已联网并且有足够的存储空间，然后再点一次该语言。如果仍然失败，请重启 iPhone 后再试。

应用听不到我说话。

说了么需要“麦克风”和“语音识别”权限，请在 iOS“设置”›“App”›“说了么”中打开。说话时请一直按住按钮，说完再松开。

提示“没听清，请再试一次”。

按住按钮，靠近手机清楚地说话，然后松开。语音识别由 Apple 提供，部分语言需要联网。

怎么听译文？

点译文旁边的喇叭图标即可。如果想自动朗读每条译文，请在应用的“设置”中打开“自动朗读翻译”。如果听不到声音，请检查 iPhone 是否静音、音量是否调高。

怎么更改应用的语言？

在应用的“设置”中选择“应用语言”，也可以在 iOS“设置”›“App”›“说了么”›“语言”中设置。

怎么删除对话？

在“对话”页，把某条对话向左滑并点“删除”，或者点“全部删除”。对话只保存在你的 iPhone 上。

支持哪些语言？

英语、西班牙语、简体中文、印地语、阿拉伯语、法语、葡萄牙语（巴西）、俄语、日语、德语、韩语、意大利语、越南语、土耳其语、泰语、荷兰语、印度尼西亚语、波兰语和乌克兰语。需要运行 iOS 18 或更高版本的 iPhone。
