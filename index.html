Talk Yeah Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

This policy explains how the Talk Yeah (说了么) app for iPhone (“the app”) handles your information. We wrote it in plain language. If something is unclear, contact us.

Who we are

Talk Yeah is made by Mengxuan Lyu (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at wxyz.trt@gmail.com.

Summary

  • There are no accounts, and we don’t run any servers. We don’t receive your conversations, your voice, or your settings.
  • The app has no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking, and it contains no third-party analytics or advertising code.
  • Translation runs on your iPhone, using Apple’s built-in Translation framework.
  • Your conversations are saved only on your iPhone. You can delete them at any time.
  • Some data can leave your iPhone, but it goes to Apple, not to us:
    • iOS downloads Apple’s translation languages from Apple when you first use them.
    • iOS speech recognition may send your voice to Apple when it can’t recognize that language on the device.

What happens on your iPhone

  • Translation. Text is translated on your iPhone by Apple’s Translation framework. The text you translate is not sent to Apple or to us.
  • Speaking translations aloud. Read-aloud uses the voices built into your iPhone and runs on the device.
  • Your conversations. Conversations are stored in a database on your iPhone. Each one contains the original text, the translated text, the language codes and timestamps.
  • Your settings. Settings are stored on your iPhone too: your chosen languages, the languages you’ve used recently and how often you’ve used each one, and display and speech preferences.

What leaves your iPhone

The app’s own code makes no network requests. The following happens through Apple software built into iOS.

1. Translation language downloads (Apple)

Apple’s translation languages are downloaded in pairs. When you start a conversation, or tap a language’s download button, and the languages you need aren’t on your iPhone yet, iOS asks you to confirm and then downloads them from Apple. These are the same languages Apple’s Translate app uses, and you can manage them in iOS Settings › Apps › Translate. Like any download, this request reveals your device’s IP address and which languages were requested. Apple handles it under the Apple Privacy Policy.

2. Speech recognition (Apple)

When you hold a talk button, the app uses iOS speech recognition to turn your speech into text. When your iPhone has on-device recognition for the language you’re speaking, recognition happens on the device. Otherwise, iOS may send the audio to Apple’s servers to recognize it. The app doesn’t force on-device-only recognition, because that would stop the app from working for many languages. Apple handles this audio under the Apple Privacy Policy. For more detail, see Apple’s Siri, Dictation & Privacy page.

The app doesn’t record or save your audio. Only the recognized text is kept, as part of the conversation on your iPhone.

We don’t store anything on our own servers, because we don’t have any.

Backups: App data, including your conversations and settings, is included in iCloud and computer backups of your iPhone, like most apps’ data. Those backups are handled by Apple under your iCloud or backup settings.

Retention and deletion

  • Conversations stay on your iPhone until you delete them. You can delete a single conversation, or use Delete all conversations in Settings. Deleted text is overwritten in the database rather than just being marked as deleted.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its conversations and settings from your iPhone. Translation languages belong to iOS, not the app; remove them in iOS Settings › Apps › Translate. Copies in an existing iPhone backup stay there until that backup is replaced or deleted.
  • We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete or send you. For data held by Apple (described above), see Apple’s privacy policy for how to access or delete it.

Permissions

  • Microphone and Speech Recognition: used only while you hold a talk button, to turn your speech into text. You can turn these off at any time in iOS Settings, but then the app can’t hear you.
  • Vibration (haptics): used for small vibration feedback. It doesn’t involve any data.

The app doesn’t ask for your location, contacts, photos or any account.

Children

The app isn’t designed for children and doesn’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Since we don’t receive any personal data, we hold none about children. If you believe a child’s information has reached us, contact us and we’ll help.

Third parties

The app relies only on Apple software built into iOS: the Translation framework (translation and language downloads), speech recognition, and voices. See the Apple Privacy Policy.

We don’t sell or share your data, and we don’t use any advertising or tracking services.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s handling of data changes, for example if we add accounts or cloud sync, we’ll update this policy and change the effective date above. If a change is significant, we’ll also explain it in the app or in the app’s release notes.
